## Walkthrough questions and recorded answers

### What do we need to do to advance this work?

- Form project groups and elect a group leader.
- Define and assign roles and responsibilities.
- Submit a business case outlining project scope and objectives.
- Create a team charter to align on ways of working and team agreements.
- Set up an Agile logbook to track progress and decisions.
- Establish daily stand-ups for communication and accountability.

### Is anyone working on anything that is not on the board?

The recorded answer was **No**.

### What do we need to finish as a team?

- A defined and prioritized Product Backlog.
- UX artifacts: personas, user stories, user journey maps, and acceptance criteria.
- Mobile and dashboard wireframes.
- A designed and documented API.
- A secure backend with JWT authentication and authorization.
- Developed and tested mobile and dashboard frontends.
- End-to-end and unit testing.
- A deployed product ready for production, with one or two sign-ups.

### What bottlenecks or blockers could affect the work?

- Time constraints and fixed deadlines.
- Printing costs and access to printing resources.
- Limited mobile data affecting research and collaboration.
- Power outages.
- Burnout and fatigue.
- Skill gaps in some technical areas.
- Limited access to paid or licensed tools.
- Communication breakdowns or delays.
- Unclear requirements that could cause rework.
- Uneven workload distribution.
- Hosting costs that could exceed the team's budget.
